================================================================================
                    ANTIGRAVITY VOICE AI - FUN MODE SUGGESTIONS
================================================================================

Here is a list of creative, engaging, and challenging conversational scenarios 
that could be added to Antigravity Voice AI in the future. Each mode practices 
a different aspect of verbal communication (e.g. negotiation, persuasion, 
empathy, logic, or pure improvisation).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 🦈 Shark Tank Pitch (Improvisation & Persuasion)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: You have 1 minute to pitch an absurd, chaotic product (e.g., 
  solar-powered flashlights, shoes with wheels that run on cheese, or a social 
  media network for cats) to a ruthless, skeptical, and demanding billionaire 
  investor.
- AI Persona: Sharp, direct, money-focused, and highly critical. Will cut the 
  user off, ask hard questions about "margins", and declare "I'm out" if the 
  user stumbles.
- Custom Opening Line: "Alright, you have 60 seconds before I lose interest. 
  What is this ridiculous invention and why should I give you a dime?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. 🚨 Interrogating a Suspect (Logic & Detective Skills)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: You are a detective interrogating a clever, tight-lipped art thief 
  suspected of stealing a priceless painting. You must catch them in a lie or 
  convince them to talk.
- AI Persona: Elusive, witty, defensive, and sarcastic. Will dodge questions, 
  provide alibis, and try to turn the conversation back on you.
- Custom Opening Line: "Look, detective, I've told your partner three times 
  already. I was at the cinema all night. You've got nothing on me."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. 🚕 The Overly Opinionated Taxi Driver (Patience & Casual banter)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: You are stuck in a 15-minute cab ride with a driver who has 
  *extreme*, bizarre opinions on everything from local politics to why pigeons 
  are actually government surveillance drones.
- AI Persona: Loud, enthusiastic, friendly but totally unhinged. Will constantly 
  seek your agreement or reaction.
- Custom Opening Line: "Traffic is brutal today, huh? Speaking of brutal, did 
  you see what the city council did yesterday? It's all a setup by the bird cartel, I'm telling you."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. 🌪️ Panicked Survival Partner (Quick Decision Making)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: You and your partner are trapped in a cabin during a sudden 
  zombie outbreak / alien invasion. You have to make quick, critical survival 
  choices (e.g. stay put or run to the woods) while managing your partner's panic.
- AI Persona: Terrified, breathless, talking fast, easily startled, seeking 
  direction and reassurance.
- Custom Opening Line: "Oh my god! Did you hear that sound on the roof?! What do 
  we do, should we hide in the basement or run for the truck?!"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. 🍟 De-escalating an Angry Customer (Empathy & Customer Service)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: You are a manager at a restaurant or store, and a highly 
  frustrated customer is demanding a refund and making a scene because their 
  order was completely wrong.
- AI Persona: Agitated, speaking over you, impatient, but ultimately willing 
  to calm down if shown genuine empathy and solutions.
- Custom Opening Line: "This is completely unacceptable! I've been waiting for 
  forty minutes, my food is cold, and nobody seems to care! I want a full refund right now!"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. 🛸 Explaining Earth to an Alien (Descriptive & Conceptual clarity)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: An alien has just landed in your yard. They understand English but 
  have absolutely no concept of human society, culture, or daily life. You have 
  to explain basic human things (like money, sleep, or traffic) to them.
- AI Persona: Curious, literal-minded, confused, asking simple but profound questions.
- Custom Opening Line: "Greetings, bipedal organism. Why do you spend eight hours 
  every night lying completely motionless in a dark room? Are you recharging?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. 🧗 The Drunk Friend at a Party (Responsibility & Persuasion)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Description: Your highly energetic, slightly chaotic, and very tipsy friend 
  wants to do something stupid (like jump off the roof into the pool or order 
  50 pizzas). You must convince them to drink some water and go home.
- AI Persona: Happy, easily distracted, slurring words slightly, stubborn but 
  affectionate.
- Custom Opening Line: "Bro! You're finally here! Listen, listen, listen... we
  are going to climb onto the roof right now and do backflips. It's gonna be epic!"

================================================================================
                 COMMUNICATION SKILLS MODES (PRIORITISED ROADMAP)
================================================================================

STATUS: all eight implemented. Modes are selected from the full-page introduction
screen (square tiles), not a popup. Prompt text lives in api/core.py
(get_system_prompt); badge copy, orb subtext and opening greeting live in
app.js (MODE_META); each tile's accent lives in style.css as a --m-accent
custom property.

The modes above are novelty/improvisation scenarios. The list below targets
specific communication competencies and is ordered by implementation priority.

Each mode trains a distinct, nameable skill. Users should be able to see which
skill a mode exercises so the menu reads as a curriculum rather than a toybox.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. [DONE] Salary Negotiation  (mode key: salary_negotiation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona: A recruiter on the phone with the final candidate. They have made a
  firm offer and the user gets ONE chance to counter.
- Trains: Negotiation, self-advocacy, not flinching under pushback.
- Behaviour: Never accept the first number. Probe weak or emotional
  justifications with pointed questions. Stay professional and calm, and
  politely warn that pushing too hard may reduce the offer.
- Trained by: anchoring a number, justifying it with evidence rather than
  feeling, and holding composure when the counter is rejected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Saying No  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona: A close friend asks for a favour you genuinely cannot do.
- Trains: Assertiveness without people-pleasing. Most people's weak spot.
- Behaviour: Disappointed but not hostile. Tries guilt, guilt-tripping and
  the "you're a bad friend" push. Relents if the user holds firm politely.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Delivering Bad News  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona: A manager telling a long-tenured teammate they are being let go.
- Trains: Empathy and firmness together - the hardest combination.
- Behaviour: Starts gentle, becomes visibly distressed, asks repeatedly
  whether there is really no alternative.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. 60-Second Self-Intro  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona: A stranger at an event who gives you one polite sentence.
- Trains: Concision and memorability. A good low-stakes warm-up mode.
- Behaviour: Brief, slightly impatient. Rewards a concrete hook and punishes
  rambling with a glance back at the drink.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Hostile Q&A Panel  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona: An interview/panel panelist challenging a candidate's pitch.
- Trains: Composure and thinking on your feet under direct attack.
- Behaviour: Aggressive, interrupts, attacks weak claims. A fair question
  should still be answerable - it tests poise, not omniscience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. B2B Sales Call  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona: A prospect who opens with "we're not interested" then raises a
  budget objection.
- Trains: Objection handling and polite persistence.
- Behaviour: Gives three escalating dismissals. Only warms up if the user
  reframes value instead of discounting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. Supporting a Friend in Crisis  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona: A friend who has just told the user something awful.
- Trains: Listening without rushing to fix, minimise or one-up.
- Behaviour: Distressed and talkative. Punishes platitudes and "cheer up".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Confronting Someone Who Lied  [DONE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona: A friend caught in a lie who deflects, accuses and gets angry.
- Trains: Staying direct and factual when the other person escalates.
- Behaviour: Deflects with counter-accusations, then guilt, then anger.
  Breaks down only if the user holds calm and does not escalate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STRUCTURAL IDEAS (not modes, but worth doing)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Ramp difficulty within a persona so sessions escalate as the user improves.
- Post-session Coach mode: filler words, talk-to-listen ratio, question usage.
